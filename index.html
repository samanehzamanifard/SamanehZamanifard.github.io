Research agenda

Two lines of work, one on people and one on models, that meet in reasoning-aware AI tutors.

How learners engage with AI explanations

Eye tracking and pupillometry show how personalized explanations change where learners look, without longer viewing time.

How language models organize reasoning

Across 30,400 solutions from eight models, internal activations cluster by reasoning approach rather than topic.

Reasoning-aware AI tutors

Tutors that adapt guidance to a learner's evolving understanding, built on the two lines of work beside it.

Tiger AI, Untwist, GENIUS, EngageAgent
