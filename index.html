Samaneh Zamanifard, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Research Associate
AI4STEM Education Center, University of Georgia

My research examines how the design of AI-supported and immersive systems shapes learning, attention, and social interaction. I develop and evaluate interactive systems using qualitative research, quantitative modeling, and eye-tracking methods.

My current work focuses on LLM-supported learning and learner modeling, building on research in social virtual reality, privacy, and embodied interaction. I earned my Ph.D. in Human-Centered Computing from Clemson University, advised by Andrew Robb, where I was a Meta Research PhD Fellow (2020–2022).

Research Interests

  • Artificial Intelligence in Learning
  • Personalized LLMs
  • Immersive & Mixed Reality Training
  • Human-Centered Computing
