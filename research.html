AI & Large Language Models

LLM-Powered Personalized Learning Lead

Nov 2024 – 2026
Clemson University
  • Built Tiger AI, a research platform integrating GPT-4o, LLaMA 3, and Gemini 2 Pro for interactive learning experiments.
  • Built a dashboard for configuring surveys and designing LLM-driven learning experiments.
  • Led a study with 1,317 students showing that LLMs predict learning styles poorly zero-shot but improve with few-shot prompting and fine-tuning.

LLM Adoption in Higher Education Lead

Jan – Nov 2024
Clemson University
  • Led a large-scale study with 900+ undergraduates using advanced quantitative methods to analyze perceptions of LLMs.
  • 67–72% of students reported improved comprehension, while only 24–30% reported high reliance on LLMs for academic success.
  • Conscientiousness reduced perceived dependence, while extraversion and neuroticism correlated with perceived learning benefits.

Untwist: AI-Driven Interactive Video Learning

Ongoing
Clemson University
  • Developed Untwist, combining GPT-4o and computer vision for real-time interactive video learning.
  • Designed a framework where learners select regions of a video and receive context-aware AI responses.

  • Showed feasibility for education and corporate training, addressing the limits of passive video learning.
